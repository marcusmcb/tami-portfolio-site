About

Experience leadership for human-AI partnership

For more than 20 years, I've led teams building complex products at the intersection of technology, people, and systems. I'm drawn to work where the stakes are high, the path isn't obvious, and clarity really matters—especially as AI becomes a deeper part of how people do their jobs.

As a leader, I'm known for bringing calm, focus, and structure to ambiguity. I believe great work comes from strong relationships, clear expectations, and environments where people feel supported, respected, and empowered to do their best thinking.

I'm at my best when I'm building teams and systems that last—helping designers grow, setting clear direction, and creating frameworks that make complex work more manageable.

I believe clarity and humanity are competitive advantages.
